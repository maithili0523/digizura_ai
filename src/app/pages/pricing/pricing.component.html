CableNXT ERP — Pricing Options

Flexible pricing designed for cable manufacturers. Choose the deployment model that fits your scale, infrastructure, and growth strategy.

Cloud (SaaS)

Subscription-based ERP deployment designed for fast implementation and scalability.

  • Monthly or annual subscription
  • Pricing based on active users
  • Public cloud hosting
  • Includes updates and support
  • Rapid scaling as business grows

On-Premise Deployment

Enterprise deployment for organizations requiring internal hosting and infrastructure control.

  • One-time license per module
  • Internal infrastructure hosting
  • Full control over data and system environment
  • Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)

Implementation & Add-Ons

Implementation scope depends on project size, customization needs, and operational requirements.

  • Implementation fees vary by scope
  • Data migration billed separately
  • Customization available if required
  • AI modules available as optional add-ons

Flexible Pricing Strategy

Pricing is aligned with the operational scale and module requirements of cable manufacturers.

Organizations can combine per-user licensing for organization-wide adoption with per-module deployment for phased rollout.
