Inventory & Warehouse Management

Inventory & Warehouse Management delivers real-time visibility and control over all material movements—from raw material receipt through work-in-process tracking to finished goods storage. This integrated system links Goods Receipt Notes directly to Quality Control, manages multi-location warehousing, and provides comprehensive aging analysis to optimize working capital and prevent material obsolescence.

Goods Receipt (GRN)

Streamlined receiving process with automatic PO matching, weight capture, and instant QC integration for rapid material availability.

Inventory Management

Comprehensive stock control across raw materials, WIP, finished goods, and scrap with real-time aging reports and valuation.

Warehouse Operations

Efficient material handling with bin location management, picking optimization, and automated stock transfer workflows.
